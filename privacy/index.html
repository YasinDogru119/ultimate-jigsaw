Ultimate Jigsaw Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Ultimate Jigsaw Puzzle app ("the app") for Android handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by Y2 Soft ("we", "us"). If a translation differs from this English version, the English version prevails.

1. Summary

  • The app has no accounts. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or precise location.
  • Your puzzles, progress, settings and photos stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to measure usage and to fix crashes. These Google services collect some device and usage data, described below.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your game progress, points and league, levels, saved puzzles, favourites, tool balances (magnets, hints, previews), purchases status and settings (language, theme, sound, music, vibration) are saved locally on your device. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. Camera and photos

You can make a puzzle from your own picture. The app accesses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to, and only the picture you pick. The picture is processed on your device and, if you leave a puzzle unfinished, a copy is kept in the app's private storage so you can continue. Your photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else. You can refuse or withdraw the camera/photo permission at any time in your phone settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

4. Data collected by third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information (model, operating system, language), IP address (from which an approximate location such as country or city may be derived) and your interactions with ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for ages 13+. Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch them in exchange for in-game tools or bonus points.

Google Firebase Analytics. Records how the app is used, for example screens opened, puzzles started or finished, tools used, and whether a rewarded ad was watched, together with an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version, language and country (derived from IP address). We use this only in aggregate to improve the game.

Google Firebase Crashlytics. If the app crashes, a crash report is sent containing technical information (device model, operating system, app version, the state of the app and the error). It contains no photos or personal content.

Google Firebase Remote Config. Lets us adjust game settings (for example how many free tools you receive) without an app update. It uses an app-instance identifier.

Google Play. If you buy "Unlimited" (unlimited hints, magnets and previews), the payment is processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid.

Google acts under its own terms and policies: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Why we use this data (legal bases)

  • To run the app and its features, and to process purchases (performance of a contract).
  • To show ads that fund the free app, and to measure and improve the app and fix crashes (your consent where required by law, otherwise our legitimate interest in providing and improving a free app).

6. Your consent and choices

  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent dialog (Google's consent tool) when the app starts. Personalised ads and analytics storage are used only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Notifications (for example league reminders) are created on your device and can be turned off in your phone settings.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data is shared only with the Google services listed above, which process it to provide their services to us, and when required by law.

8. Retention

  • Data on your device: until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • AdMob and Google Play: according to Google's policies linked above.

9. International transfers and security

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

10. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold accounts or contact details, the simplest way to delete everything is to uninstall the app; Google-held analytics data can be removed on request — contact us and include, if possible, the approximate date you used the app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

13. Contact
